# Brag Plan: Jituri Furnitures

## What is this app?
A digital flagship and dynamic catalog platform for Jituri Furnitures, showcasing bespoke handcrafted furniture crafted across two decades in Belagavi, powered by React, Vite, Express, and WebAssembly SQLite.

## The angle
High craftsmanship meets modern high-speed engineering. Two decades of timber mastery and over 1,000 bespoke interior commissions brought to life through a cinematic, ultra-responsive digital catalog.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
Deep obsidian widescreen letterbox. A golden typography reveal strikes the beat:
**"SINCE 2005. 21 YEARS OF TIMBER MASTERY."**

## Key moments (the middle)
- **The Triad of Craft:** Woodworking. Cushioning. Polishing. All under one roof in Belagavi.
- **The Living Catalog:** Curated collection cards with fluid hover states, client-side compressed imagery, and high-fidelity lightboxes.
- **The Zero-Bloat Core:** WebAssembly SQLite database operating at native speed with zero compiler overhead.

## Outro / punchline
The camera centers on the glowing brand monogram:
**"JITURI FURNITURES — Built for permanence. Delivered to the world."**

## User flow worth showing
1. **Entry & Discovery:** Widescreen brand banner transitions into Curated Collections.
2. **Key Action:** Seamless album card focus and high-resolution modal inspection.
3. **Power Backend:** Admin drag-and-drop asset ingestion and live catalog sync.
